## 2. RentCast — three data types, kept apart (GENERATED from `RENTCAST_DATA_TYPES`)

The directive's rule, now in one table every reader derives from (`lib/market-reports/contract.ts`):

- `/v1/properties` carries the **sale history** (`lastSaleDate`, `lastSalePrice`, `history[*].event "Sale"`) and, with `saleDateRange`, is an **area sold-records** filter — the recorded-sale evidence, and the comps source when the tenant has no connected IDX.
- `/v1/markets` is **ZIP-level sale and rental LISTING statistics** (asking prices, listing counts, days on market) — not a closed-sale index. The writer's column name `market_data.median_sale_price` is historical; every reader re-labels it an asking price.
- `/v1/listings/sale` is **listing records** that need provider rights and freshness and carry **no photo** — a listing fact field grants no photo right. The MLS name on the row is shown as the listing's source; the status is as RentCast reports it (`provider_reported`), never "verified active".
- `/v1/avm/value` comparables are **listing comparables**: the price is the comparable's listing-record price and `removedDate` means it left the market — not a recorded deed.

Every result is labelled by source and verified-status: an in-house row is the tenant's own listing; an IDX row is `feed_active` (the feed's active set; photos render live from the feed and are never copied); a RentCast row is `provider_reported` with the MLS name shown as the source and no photo. Unverified listing status and licensed photography are never misrepresented: the only lawful photo sources remain the tenant's IDX/MLS feed and the tenant user's own attested uploads.

| dataAge | a record's data age (last_verified_at / signal_at, lib/lead-pipeline/freshness.ts) is frozen at cohort draw; every arm sees the same age |

**Blinded verification:** sample share 0.2 — a 20% random sample per cohort is verified for right-party accuracy against contact points the platform ALREADY holds with consent (the buyer_candidate truth set, consented inbound records); the verifier sees provider answers with the provider name REMOVED. Lawful: phone / email usability is judged only where a lawful basis to contact exists (TCPA / CAN-SPAM; the DNC gate decides) — no verification call is placed to a person

**Measured:**

- match rate (hits ÷ attempted) per cohort per arm
- field accuracy on the blinded sample (right-party verified ÷ checked)
- phone / email usability when lawful (non-DNC mobile or deliverable email that is the right person)
- owner / property ambiguity (answers naming more than one owner, or an owner not on the record)
- data refresh age (the vendor's dated fields where published; otherwise 'unpublished')
- cost per usable unique person (true marginal cost: misses free on per-match products; retries billed)
- incremental overlap beyond the incumbents (a field the incumbents already hold is not new coverage)
- signal usefulness: seller / refinance propensity evidence in the VENDOR'S OWN unit, observed against 90-day outcomes — never a probability, never cross-ranked
- read / write / update capabilities (batch read, single read, push / monitor, update of a held record)
- SaaS / multi-tenant LICENSE (written platform use, storage on tenant records, end-user delivery, white-label terms)
